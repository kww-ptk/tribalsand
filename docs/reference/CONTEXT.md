TribalSand is a premium, sustainable coastal hospitality brand in Kenya combining beachfront stays, wellness driven lifestyle venues and experiences with measurable environmental impact into one integrated beachfront ecosystem that connects like-minded travellers with eco-conscious luxury, wellbeing experiences and unforgettable oceanfront escapes.

- Seek privacy, comfort and authentic coastal life
- Value sustainability and environmental responsibility
- Want creative community hubs that balance comfort, adventure and authenticity
- Are wealthy yet environmentally aware — they expect both privacy and responsibility

## 2. BRAND VOICE & WRITING RULES

- **Direct and specific.** No superlatives for the sake of them. No "world-class" or "unparalleled".
- **No "barefoot luxury".** This phrase is banned. It is overused and generic.
- **No "luxury" as a standalone adjective unless paired with something specific.** Say "luxury beachfront boutique hotel" not just "luxury hotel".
- **Confident, not boastful.** State facts. Let the property speak.
- **Short sentences.** Especially for key statements.
- **Use em dashes (—) not hyphens for parenthetical statements.**
- **Sustainability is infrastructure, not marketing.** Present it as how the property works, not a badge.
- **"Tribe" is deliberate.** A tribe shares values, a sense of place and a way of moving through the world.

### Approved Phrases
- "Kenya as it was meant to be experienced."
- "Like-minded travellers"
- "Interconnected sustainable coastal system"
- "Beachfront village"
- "Your stay is powered by the sun."
- "Luxury without freshwater depletion."
- "Join a community of like-minded travellers who value good work, good waves and good company."
- "Your stay supports local people and projects that keep this coastline thriving."

## 5. TRIBAL DUNES — KILIFI ECOSYSTEM

### What Is Tribal Dunes
Tribal Dunes is one large beachfront property in Kilifi containing:
- Maya Kobe luxury beachfront boutique hotel
- Maya Ilai eco retreat compound (at the back, walking distance to beach through Somewhere Café)
- Off Duty beachfront coworking hotel
- Somewhere Café (at Off Duty)
- Tribal Table restaurant (next to Maya Kobe)
- Tribal Kite School Kilifi (directly in front of Off Duty)

All venues are within walking distance of each other.

### Tribal Dunes Positioning
An integrated sustainable beachfront village built around adventure, community and love for nature. Built for like-minded individuals — a collection of meeting spots, activity areas, accommodations, restaurants and coworking spaces.

### Tribal Dunes Sustainability Infrastructure
- **Fully solar powered** — all venues run on solar energy
- **Desalinated ocean water system** — no reliance on Kenya's freshwater reserves
- **Rainwater harvesting and management**
- **Coral growing garden** — active reef restoration project to replant coral in the reef
- Talking point: "Your stay is powered by the sun."
- Talking point: "Luxury without freshwater depletion."

### Tribal Dunes Strategic Positioning
An integrated sustainable beachfront village built around wellbeing, community and coastal living in comfort, with a range of accommodation offerings for different types of travellers.

## 17. GEOGRAPHIC CONTEXT

### Watamu
- Coastal town, Kilifi County
- Watamu Marine Park and Marine National Reserve (UNESCO)
- Famous for white sand beaches, snorkelling, sea turtles
- ~120km north of Mombasa
- Malindi Airport (MYD) — closest airport

### Kilifi
- Coastal town, Kilifi County
- Kilifi Creek — famous for sailing and dhow cruises
- Bofa Beach — location of Tribal Dunes
- Growing creative and digital nomad community
- ~60km north of Mombasa

### Vipingo
- Small coastal area between Kilifi and Mombasa
- Vipingo Ridge — 18-hole PGA golf course
- ~30km north of Mombasa
- Mombasa Moi Airport (MBA) — 45 minutes away
- Very private and exclusive coastal strip

### Airport Options
- Malindi Airport (MYD) — closest to Watamu
- Mombasa Moi International (MBA) — main hub, closest to Vipingo
- Nairobi JKIA (NBO) — main international gateway, then domestic flight or road
- Wilson Airport (WIL) — Nairobi domestic, for charter flights
