Open till — three designs

Same screen as today (Shop outlet, Patrik in-house at Maya Ilai), redrawn three ways in Inter. Pick one and I'll build it into the real till; every function stays as it is.

A · Calm light. A slim icon rail for the outlets, big photo cards, and a white order panel with quantity steppers. Pick a payment method, then one teal button that says the amount. The closest to today, just cleaner and roomier.

B · Dark bar & receipt. Outlets become tabs in a dark top bar, categories sit in a left list with counts, and items are compact rows, so more fit on screen. The order panel reads like a printed receipt, with Room charge as the big option for in-house guests.

C · Bento cards. Everything sits in rounded cards on a soft grey background, like the admin Dashboard. Large touch tiles for items, and the customer, order and payment in separate cards. The total and the pay button sit in a dark card at the bottom right.
