Settings — 3 designs
Change anything — the one Save bar appears. Sample data.

A · Section menu. One page in clear sections (Booking, Guests & email, Money, Messages, Website, Your account), with a small menu on the left that jumps to each and lights up as you scroll. Each setting has a plain title and one line of explanation. Change anything and a dark bar slides up: “3 changes — Booking form mode, Notification email… · Discard · Save changes”. One button saves all of them.

B · Label + control rows. The calm “settings app” look: the explanation sits on the left, the control on the right, one row per setting, grouped under small headings. A changed row gets a soft gold edge so you can see what you touched before saving. Save sits both at the top of the page (always visible, greyed out until something changes) and in the bar at the bottom.

C · Overview that opens. Short page: each group is one line showing what is set right now (“Enquiry mode · website holds off · units auto”). Tap a group to open it in place and edit; groups you changed show “2 changed”. The same single Save bar saves every open or closed group at once.
