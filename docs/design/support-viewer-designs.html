Mockup — the real page draws the PDF's own pages (pdf.js)

A · Clean reader. The pages scroll on a warm background with soft shadows, like reading on paper — no grey browser toolbar. Thumbnails down the left (click to jump), a thin progress line on top, and one floating control: previous / page 3 of 17 / next, zoom, fit to width, download. Scrolling updates the page number.

B · Chapters. Reads like a course: the guide's chapters on the left (from the PDF's bookmarks or headings) with the page each starts on, one page at a time on the right with big previous / next arrows, a "Section · page" line on top, and a reading-progress bar. Keyboard ← → turns pages. Best for long guides people work through.

C · Book spread. Two pages side by side like an open book on a dark, quiet background, with a filmstrip of every page underneath. Turning a page flips the spread. Feels premium and suits a full-screen "read the guide" moment; on a phone it shows one page.
