Mockup — click a guide to view it, Download, or Import (owner)

A · Library shelf. A grid of guide covers like your PDF list, grouped "For your team" / "For guests & partners", with search and a filter per role. A cover opens the guide in a side viewer; every card has View and Download. The owner gets Import guide (side panel: drop the PDF, title, who it's for, new guide or new version of an existing one).

B · Reader. Built for reading: the list of guides on the left, the selected guide open on the right, page by page. Download and Version history (older versions stay downloadable) sit above the guide. The owner imports from the small drop box at the top of the list or uploads a new version of the open guide.

C · Support hub. Starts with the guide for your role (Reception sees the Reception Guide first), then every guide in one table: who it's for, version, last updated, size, View / Download. The owner drags PDFs straight onto the import box; the table rows also offer New version and Remove.
