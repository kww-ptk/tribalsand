About the Property

Vipingo's Best-Kept Secret

Along the north coast of Kenya, overlooking the Indian Ocean, lies Vipingo's best-kept secret — My Amani. A tastefully furnished five-bedroom retreat with endless views of the Indian Ocean at one end and lush indigenous gardens at the other.

My Amani was recycled from an existing home and renovated with the finest local craftsmanship — lying on an eco-friendly beach that protects endangered sea turtle nesting grounds. The entire property is available exclusively as a full private retreat.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Check-in & check-out times? +
Check-in from 2:00 PM, check-out by 10:00 AM. Early or late requests accommodated where possible — contact us in advance.
Is a private chef available? +
Yes, on request at an additional cost. Please request when booking. The kitchen is fully equipped for self-catering if preferred.
Entire property only or individual rooms? +
My Amani is available exclusively as a full private retreat. The entire five-bedroom property is yours — it is not available for partial bookings.
Are children welcome? +
Absolutely. My Amani is family-friendly with ample space. Babysitting services available on request through your concierge.
Airport transfers? +
Yes, arranged at additional cost. Provide flight details in advance. We service Mombasa Moi (MBA), Malindi (MYD) and Nairobi JKIA (NBO).
Minimum stay? +
Standard and mid-season: 2 nights minimum. Peak season: 5 nights minimum. A USD 500 security deposit applies.
